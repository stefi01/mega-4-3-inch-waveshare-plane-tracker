Flash Download Tool 3.9.3. These are the boxes in that program.
This is not the Arduino IDE Tools menu.

Open flash_download_tool_3.9.3.exe.

First window
  WorkMode: Develop
  LoadMode: UART
  ChipType: ESP32-S3
  Click OK.

Big window, SPIDownload tab
  SPI SPEED: 80MHz
  SPI MODE: DIO
  DoNotChgBin: ticked
  LockSettings: left unticked
  BAUD: 115200

Tick all four rows. The files are in this same folder.

  tick  mega_43.ino.bootloader.bin    address 0x0
  tick  mega_43.ino.partitions.bin    address 0x8000
  tick  boot_app0.bin                 address 0xe000
  tick  mega_43.ino.esp32s3.bin       address 0x10000

Do not press ERASE.
COM is the CH340 port. Cable in the Type-C socket. No UART switch. Leave BOOT alone.
Click START. Wait until the box says FINISH.
