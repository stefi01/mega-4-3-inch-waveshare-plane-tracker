Mega 4.3 version 1.0. Published 23 Nov 2026.
CHECK reads ota/version_43.txt. INSTALL reads ota/mega43.bin.
The app bin for the flash download tool is sketch\mega_43\mega_43.ino.esp32s3.bin at address 0x10000.
Field lists 6 planes per page and has a NEXT PAGE button. Traffic lists 8 per page.
A landing city is dropped when the airplane is moving and that airport is more than 90 degrees off the nose.
This is the Freenove FNK0115B, 4.3 inch, 480x272. It is not the Waveshare 7 inch.

Sketch: mega_43\sketch\mega_43\mega_43.ino
The 7 inch sketch was not edited. Final_3.5.ino was not edited.

Board, leave these alone
Clock 9000000. Bounce 272 * 20. Rotation 2.
Porches H 0, 8, 4, 43 and V 0, 8, 4, 12.
Pins DE 40, VSYNC 41, HSYNC 39, PCLK 42.
Red 45, 48, 47, 21, 14. Green 5, 6, 7, 15, 16, 4. Blue 8, 3, 46, 9, 1.
Backlight is GPIO 2, analogWrite. There is no CH422 lamp on this board.
Touch is XPT2046. SPI.begin before ts.begin. Rotation 0.
Two samples, 4 ms apart. Drop them if they differ by more than 20.
Raw map x 3800 to 170, y 250 to 3800, then flip both axes.
Map photos are copied in 1024-byte chunks, drawn twice, and thrown away if the two signatures differ.
This sketch does not restart the panel after Wi-Fi.

Product
Version 1.0. Prefs name mega43, so Factory does not clear the old Home Field Scope or the 7 inch Mega.
Updates: https://github.com/stefi01/mega-4-3-inch-waveshare-plane-tracker
CHECK reads ota/version_43.txt. INSTALL reads ota/mega43.bin.
Do not upload the 7 inch ws7.bin onto this glass.
Range is 10 to 250 NM, step 10, default 50. 50 NM is the radius, so the circle is 100 NM across.
Ships are optional and start off. Green triangles sit on the same rings as the planes.
Location is a 5-digit zip or a 4-letter airport code. CLEAR wipes the pad. DEL removes one character.
The clock uses the time zone already saved for that place. There is no extra summer-time hour and no SUMMER or WINTER chip.
SAT, STREET, zoom, drag, list, detail, night sky, ISS, launches, storm, and the screen saver are still in the sketch.
